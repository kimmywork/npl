## C2b direct entries and call sites (perf)

User go after C2a. Design: `docs/design-calls.md` C2 addendum (E1-E3).

- `frontend/direct-calls.npl`: `direct-calls-analyze!` (replaces the
  report entry; report still under `NPL_DIRECT_REPORT`) builds a hashed
  table; hooks `direct-target`, `direct-safe-name?`,
  `control-synchronous-call?`, `control-primitive-or-direct?`. A
  direct-safe function converts to its CPS definition (with direct calls
  disabled, so the fallback never re-enters a direct entry) and a form
  218 with the body in direct style (lambdas still CPS).
- `frontend/control.npl`: `control-simple?` and `control-cps-application`
  treat a direct call with simple operands as synchronous, so control
  conversion no longer splits at it. `control-program.npl` emits both
  definitions.
- Backend: `program.npl` `emit-direct-function` emits
  `$_npl_dfn_<name>(l p0, ...)`: stack check against `npl_stack_limit`,
  below it `npl_call_sync` of the CPS entry; body in value position;
  self tail call is the O2 jump (`emit-hoisted-body`, shared with
  `emit-self-loop-body`; allocs hoisted to `@start`). `state.npl`
  `DIRECT-ENTRY`, `direct-function-symbol`; `tail.npl` only self jumps
  leave value position in a direct entry; `expression.npl`
  `route-call` -> `direct-call`; `environment.npl` lambdas reset
  `DIRECT-ENTRY`.
- Runtime: new `runtime/stack.c` (`npl_stack_limit`, set in
  `npl_runtime_init` from `getrlimit(RLIMIT_STACK)`, at least a quarter
  of the stack left free, at most 256 KiB of direct frames,
  `NPL_DIRECT_STACK_BYTES`). Cap because every collection scans the whole
  C stack: fixture at a 4 KiB nursery 1.2 s (64 KiB cap), 3.6 s (256 KiB),
  13 s (1 MiB), 71 s (6 MiB); at the 1 MiB default nursery 0.26-0.55 s.
- Fixture `tests/r7rs/fixtures/direct-calls` (native, npli, Guile): fib,
  tak, 1,000,000-deep `len`/`sum-list`/`count-up` (past the limit), guard
  around primitive errors shallow and 1,000,000 deep, bignum/flonum slow
  paths, call/cc escape and re-entry around direct calls, closures from
  direct functions, mutual tail recursion (no direct entry).
  `check_full_compiler.sh`: `_npl_dfn_fib` calls itself and loads the
  limit, the CPS entry makes no direct call, `my-even?` has no direct
  entry, fixture output at a 64 KiB nursery.

## C2b follow-ups before C2c: fallback windows, loop registers (perf)

Root causes of the C2b regressions (instrumented runtimes, retired
instructions from `/usr/bin/time -l`):

- fact: past the 256 KiB budget the stack stayed below the limit, so every
  direct call made by CPS code there (the library `multiply-integers` in
  the bignum slow path) fell back on its own: npl_call_sync 89,800 ->
  156,320 per run, each a nested trampoline and a `setjmp`
  (`sigprocmask` on macOS).
- loop: the direct entry copied its parameters with `copy`; QBE kept the
  loop variables in x0/x1 and added 3 register moves per iteration
  (9.02 -> 9.62 G instructions).

Fixes:

- `runtime/stack.c` `npl_direct_fallback`: the fallback opens a new window
  of `NPL_DIRECT_STACK_BYTES` below its frame (bounded by a floor that keeps
  a quarter of the stack free), so later direct calls run directly again;
  the trampoline (`NplTailContext.stack_limit`, restored on a primitive
  error) and call/cc escape frames restore the limit they saved.
- `backend/program.npl`: parameter copies are emitted after the body is
  known (`emit-hoisted-body` guard is now a procedure of whether a self
  jump occurred). With a self jump each parameter is `or`ed with
  `$npl_opaque_zero`, which QBE cannot fold, so the loop variables get
  their own registers; without one a plain `copy` (the `or` costs fib
  +16%). An assigned parameter's cell is made after the copies
  (`emit-direct-body`).

## C2c raise and variadic direct entries (perf)

Measurement first: allowing `raise` alone changed no verdict (compiler
563/927 both ways), because `error` and `argument-error` call the variadic
`list` or are variadic. So C2c covers both:

- `frontend/direct-calls.npl`: `(raise x)` is allowed (`raise-continuable`
  and other arities stay unsafe); variadic functions are analyzed (entry
  field `rest`, `direct-accepts?`); a variadic self tail call is a
  tail-cycle (no jump). The direct body turns `raise` into
  `npl_raise_direct`; formals of a variadic entry become (230 fixed rest).
  New `direct-entry-for`, `direct-rest-count`.
- `backend`: variadic direct entries take the rest list as the last C
  parameter; call sites cons it (`direct-call-ops`, `direct-rest-ops`); the
  fallback spreads it (`npl_direct_fallback_rest`). `emit-value-call` (numeric
  alias slow path) calls the library procedure's direct entry when it has
  one.
- Runtime: `npl_raise_direct` (`error.c`, delivered like a primitive error)
  and `npl_direct_fallback_rest` (`stack.c`); row `npl_raise_direct`
  (internal, not exported by `(npl primitive)`).
- Tests: probe cases uses-raise (safe), rest-count (safe), rest-loop
  (tail-cycle); fixture cases for raise/error shallow and 1,000,000 deep
  under guard, raise-continuable, call/cc escape from a handler, and
  variadic calls with 0, 1, many rest arguments, apply, bignum operands;
  `check_full_compiler.sh` checks the variadic entry signature and
  `npl_raise_direct`, and that fib's CPS entry does not call fib's direct
  entry (other direct entries may be called there: they check the stack).

Verdicts: compiler 576/941 safe (was 563/927); fact program 151/241 (was
