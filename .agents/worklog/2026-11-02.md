- `src/compiler/frontend/control.npl` `control-cps-sequence`: a simple
  non-final expression of a sequence (single-value primitive application,
  literal, lambda) is evaluated in place, as an operand is, and the
  sequence continues in the same function; the continuation-closure path
  moved to `control-cps-sequence-step`. A loop body
  `(begin (vector-set! ...) (loop ...))` keeps the loop call in the loop

## C2a direct-call analysis and numbers (report only)

- Decisions (user, 2026-11-02): C2 addendum accepted with the recommended
  E1-E3 (exclude error/raise in v1, stack limit from getrlimit, C2a first
  then go/no-go); `docs/design-calls.md` updated.
- New `src/compiler/frontend/direct-calls.npl` (325 lines): on the lowered
  core forms, before control conversion, and only with
  `NPL_DIRECT_REPORT` set, classifies each top-level function and writes
  `direct-calls: <verdict> <name>` lines and a total to the error port.
  Safe: fixed parameters, body of literals, variables, if, let forms,
  begin, set!, lambda creation, single-value primitives, numeric aliases,
  and calls to safe functions with their arity; greatest fixed point; no
  cycle of tail calls through another function (self tail calls are
  jumps). Verdicts otherwise: variadic, control, values-primitive,
  local-call, global-call, named-let, computed-call, arity,
  library-call, calls-unsafe, tail-cycle. Reuses control.npl predicates
  (`control-transfer-name?`, `control-single-value-row?`,
  `control-function-form?`) and `core-body-expression`.
- `backend/program.npl` `emit-program` calls it between lowering and
  control conversion; emission is unchanged.
- Test `src/compiler/tests/direct-calls/probe.npl` + `expected.txt` (19
  cases: fib, tak, len, mutual tail recursion, call/cc, closure call,
  closure creation, apply, error, dynamic-wind, parameterize, map,
  variadic, named let, set!); `check_full_compiler.sh` compares the
  verdicts and checks the SSA is identical with and without the report.

Numbers: workloads (user functions): fib, tak, loop, fact, build, sum,
compose safe; every `bench-main` is `named-let` (or `library-call` in
fact), find-first `control`. Compiler: 898 functions, 545 safe, 344
calls-unsafe (mostly through library procedures), 3 local-call, 3
