## 13:37:01 CST

Completed the root-cause investigation and fix for the intermittent numeric oracle abort. AddressSanitizer isolated a deterministic heap-buffer-overflow in `runtime/numeric.c:integer_to_string_radix`: the conversion buffer started at capacity 32 and grew while emitting digits, but the negative sign was appended without checking capacity. A 32-digit negative exact integer therefore wrote one byte past the allocation; the failure appeared intermittent because the reference batch only reached the triggering value depending on allocation/layout. The earlier fraction-result rooting fix was retained.
